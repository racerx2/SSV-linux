Space Shuttle Vessel 1.16 for Linux (x64), port 1.0
====================================================

An unofficial Linux build of Space Shuttle Vessel (SSV) for the native Linux Orbiter
(github.com/racerx2/orbiter-2027-linux) with its Vulkan graphics client.
The SSV team does not support this build: report problems with it to racerx2, not to them.

Space Shuttle Vessel is by the SSV team (GLS and indy91, github.com/GLS-SSV/SSV), based on
Space Shuttle Ultra. See the manual (CREDITS) for everyone whose work it contains: PEG MFD,
Attitude MFD, Usonian's VandenbergAFB-2006, Fred18's GeneralVehicle, Project Apollo - NASSP,
and the cJSON library by Dave Gamble and contributors.
Linux port: racerx2, 2026.

Licence: GNU GPL version 2 or later (SSV-LICENSE.txt). The source of this build, with every
Linux change marked "not upstream", is the "linux" branch of github.com/racerx2/SSV-linux.


Installation
------------
1. Close Orbiter.
2. Extract the zip into your Orbiter folder. It adds files only; it never replaces a file of yours.
3. From the Orbiter folder run once:
       python3 "Install/Space Shuttle Vessel/ssv_linux_setup.py"
   It adds to Config/Earth.cfg the SSV bases (DIR Earth\SSV CONTEXT SSV) and the 12 KSC
   ground cameras, adds SSV\VAFB\DeltaBlue to Config/Base.cfg, and copies the SSV terrain tiles
   (runway and pad flattening, Vandenberg imagery) into Textures/Earth. It keeps a .ssv-backup copy of each
   .cfg it changes. A tile it replaces is kept in Install/Space Shuttle Vessel/Earth-backup, and if
   the tile stream had written it, the stream leaves the new one alone from then on.
   Running it again changes nothing. It also reports settings SSV needs (see below).
4. Start Orbiter and pick a scenario under "Space Shuttle Vessel".

Differences from the Windows release
- The fonts SSV_Font_A and SSV_Font_B need no installing: SSV loads them from
  Install/Space Shuttle Vessel when it starts.
- Config/Earth.cfg and Config/Base.cfg are not overwritten (step 3 adds SSV's lines to yours).
- The Antelope Valley texture pack is not needed: the tile stream provides the imagery.
- The Mission Editor (Windows only, .NET) is not included. The missions in Missions/SSV work as they are.
- The displays are drawn with Sketchpad, as with the D3D9 client on Windows.
- With Orbiter's Collision module: the launch pads, the VAB and the LCC are fixed structures
  (FixedStructure = TRUE in their Config/Vessels files), so the stack never moves them and the
  streamed 3D buildings and trees stay off them. The pad and MLP umbilicals that let go of the
  vehicle at liftoff do not collide (the .col files next to their meshes in Meshes/SSV).

Additions in this build
- OPS 1 aborts: ATO and AOA (ABORT MODE rotary and ABORT push button, SPEC 51 OVERRIDE), the abort
  propellant dumps and the OMS/RCS interconnect.
- The shuttle-era pads LC-39A, LC-39B and SLC-6 are kept clear of streamed buildings and trees, so
  today's towers in the map data do not show (BEGIN_CLEARZONES in Config/Earth/SSV/Canaveral.cfg and
  Vandenberg.cfg, for every SSV scenario; FixedStructureClear in the pads' Config/Vessels files).
- Materials (Config/GC/SSV_OV.cfg and SSV_SRB.cfg, written by linux/ssv_materials.py): glass shine on
  the MDU screens and the windshield; the HUD combiners glow green while their HUD POWER switch is on
  and with its brightness, seen through the windshield from outside; a satin sheen on the SRB paint
  (Orbiter Linux client).
- The cockpit textures stsvc01-03.dds are repaired: the purple DXT1 specks on dark surfaces such as
  the window posts are gone (linux/ssv_texfix.py).

Settings
- Launchpad > Visual effects > Local light sources: on (the SSV lights need them).
- MFD texture size 512 (the default).
- Recommended: Launchpad > Video > Advanced > "Disable near clip plane compatibility mode".

Removing
- Delete the folders and files the zip added, put back Config/Earth.cfg and Config/Base.cfg from
  their .ssv-backup copies, and the tiles from Install/Space Shuttle Vessel/Earth-backup.
